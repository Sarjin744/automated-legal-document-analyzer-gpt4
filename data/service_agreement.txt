MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on September 1, 2026, by and between:

Client:
FinTech Global Corporation, a financial services institution headquartered at Financial Center, Mumbai - 400051 ("Client"),

AND

Service Provider:
CloudForge Engineering Solutions Pvt. Ltd., headquartered at Cyber City, Gurugram - 122002 ("Service Provider").

1. PURPOSE
The Client engages the Service Provider to perform cloud infrastructure architecture, automated deployment pipelines, and microservices modernization. The purpose is the delivery of secure enterprise cloud software according to specifications.

2. DURATION
The term of this Agreement shall commence on Start Date: 01-09-2026 and continue for a fixed duration of 6 months, ending on End Date: 28-02-2027.

3. PAYMENT AND INVOICING
• The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider a monthly fee of ₹1,50,000/month upon submission and approval of monthly sprint deliverables.
• Invoices shall be payable within 15 business days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. KEY OBLIGATIONS
• Service Provider must deliver software according to specifications and meet agreed sprint milestones.
• Service Provider shall perform rigorous automated unit and security vulnerability tests prior to code deployment.
• Service Provider must maintain 99.9% uptime for deployed continuous integration environments.
• Client must provide required API credentials and timely reviews within 5 business days of sprint reviews.

5. TERMINATION
• Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing 30 days written notice.
• Either party may terminate immediately for cause if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure within 14 days of written notice.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to treat all financial data, source repositories, and technical configurations as confidential and not disclose to third parties.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon full payment of fees, all custom source code, documentation, and architecture diagrams developed under this Agreement shall belong solely to the Client.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The aggregate liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client during the preceding three (3) months.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be construed under the laws of the State of Maharashtra, India.
