EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of July, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

Employer:
Apex Global Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered corporate office at Cyber Park, Whitefield, Bengaluru - 560066 (hereinafter referred to as the "Employer" or "Company"),

AND

Employee:
Mr. Rahul Sharma, residing at Flat 304, Silicon Residency, Electronic City, Bengaluru - 560100 (hereinafter referred to as the "Employee").

1. PURPOSE OF AGREEMENT
The Employer desires to employ the Employee, and the Employee desires to accept employment with the Employer in the capacity of Senior Software Engineer. The purpose of this agreement is the formal employment of the individual by the organization to perform designated software architecture, design, and development tasks.

2. DURATION AND TERM
The term of this Agreement shall commence on Start Date: 01-07-2026 and shall continue for an initial fixed duration of 12 months, concluding on End Date: 30-06-2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3. KEY OBLIGATIONS OF THE EMPLOYEE
• Employee must maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary information obtained during employment.
• Employee must perform assigned duties and deliver software according to specifications diligently and faithfully.
• Employee agrees to devote full business time, attention, and technical skill to the business of the Employer.
• Employee shall comply with all company compliance policies, security standards, and ethical codes of conduct.

4. PAYMENT AND COMPENSATION
• The Employer shall pay the Employee a fixed monthly salary of ₹50,000/month payable on the last working day of each calendar month.
• Compensation shall be subject to statutory tax deductions and social security withholdings as mandated by applicable law.

5. TERMINATION CONDITIONS
• Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days written notice to the other party without assigning reasons.
• The Employer reserves the right to terminate employment immediately without notice in the event of gross misconduct, breach of confidentiality, fraud, or violation of non-compete covenants.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY CLAUSE
The Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary information, software source code, customer records, and trade secrets obtained during the period of employment and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written authorization from the Employer. This obligation survives termination for a period of five (5) years.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
All inventions, source code, designs, algorithms, patents, and written documentation produced by the Employee during the course of employment shall constitute "works made for hire" and shall remain the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the Employer.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. The Employee's aggregate financial liability for gross negligence shall not exceed three months' base salary.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement shall be referred to binding arbitration in Bengaluru in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
